Load a LUT that expects Apple Log input, such as Apple’s own Apple Log to Rec. 709 LUT.

Exposure shifts the Apple Log signal before the LUT, one stop at a time. The built-in mode is a quick approximation (Apple Log decode, Rec. 2020 to Rec. 709, filmic curve) for when you have no LUT. Apple Log .mov files play in Safari; other browsers may not decode them. On iPhone, pick clips from Files rather than Photos, since Photos may convert Apple Log footage before the app sees it. 1D LUTs aren’t supported.
